# Hi, I'm Kuni! 👋

### English Pronunciation Coach | Educator | Lifelong Learner

**Helping people turn the English they know into English they can actually use.**

Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I'm an English pronunciation coach, educator, and founder of **EPC TOKYO**, an online English school based in Japan.

I believe that learning English is about more than memorizing vocabulary and grammar. It's about developing the confidence and ability to express your own thoughts, connect with others, and keep learning throughout life.

My mission is to make English learning more practical, engaging, and accessible—especially for adult learners who are ready to start again or take their skills to the next level.
